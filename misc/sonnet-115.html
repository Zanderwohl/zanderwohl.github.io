--- title: Sonnet 115 description: >- On being made to memorize poetry in twelfth grade, and Shakespeare's Sonnet 115. permalink: /misc/sonnet-115/ ---

When I was in twelfth grade, one of my English teachers got the idea that students should memorize poetry. I think this was a fantastic idea and I am grateful for being forced to do this.

That year, I memorized Shakespeare's sonnet 115, and John Donne's The Bait. I still remember both.

I recommend reading sonnets out loud; the written form is a skeleton without raiment.
