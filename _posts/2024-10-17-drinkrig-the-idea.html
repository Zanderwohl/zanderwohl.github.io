--- title: The Idea subtitle: Learning to print, prime, paint and weather, one crate at a time. description: >- Proof of concept for the drinkrig: printing industrial pieces from Thingiverse and working out a paint process that reads as rusted metal. series: Drinkrig part: 1 permalink: /posts/drinkrig-the-idea/ ---

Before I could do much, I had to learn how to do anything at all. I had no experience in model making or mini painting before, so I started with some industrial pieces. Of course, I went straight to Thingiverse to look. A crate by Printed-Toys and an electrical box by NYC3D work very well for this purpose. I also thought a drinkrig could use a crane, so I found this one by Mikesrailway. It's an old rail crane, which I think the inhabitants of a drinkrig might cannibalize for moving things around.

And with that, I had my proof of concept. Of course, after your first success, your confidence shoots up tremendously, so I went on to work on the main truss.
